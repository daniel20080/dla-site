Why it exists

Espresso machines got loud. We went quiet.

SOLSTICE is a manual lever machine milled from a single block of matte-black aluminium with a brass lever. No pump screaming at 6 a.m., no screens, no app. You heat, you pull, you drink. The whole ritual takes two minutes and makes the best thirty millilitres of your day.

01Your hand is the pump

The brass lever drives a full nine bars through the puck — pressure you can feel and shape.

02Nothing to break

Three moving parts, zero electronics. Machined aluminium, brass and steel, built for decades.

03Silent by design

The loudest thing in the room is the first sip. Pull a shot while the house sleeps.
