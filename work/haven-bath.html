How we build

Most of a bathroom is the part you never see.

Tiles last as long as what’s behind them. So the hidden work gets the most time.

  • i
    Waterproofing, tested

    Every wet area is sealed with a membrane and flood-tested before a single tile goes down.

  • ii
    Floors that fall to the drain

    A sloped screed, so water never sits on the floor.

  • iii
    New pipes, new wiring

    Supply lines and circuits replaced, not patched.

  • iv
    Heat under the stone

    Underfloor heating on its own thermostat.
